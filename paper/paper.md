# Summary

OpenFreqBench is an open-source Python framework for rigorous, reproducible benchmarking of
grid frequency estimation algorithms used in power systems monitoring.

# Statement of Need

Frequency estimators for Phasor Measurement Units (PMUs) span dozens of algorithmic families.
No unified, computationally-aware benchmark exists for comparing them under standardized conditions.
